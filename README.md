## 💡 What is Deal Stacking?

**Deal Stacking** is the practice of combining multiple independent discount layers on a single retail purchase to maximize net savings. A classic "Triple-Dip Stack" consists of:
1. **Layer 1 (Store Promo / Coupon Code):** 10%–30% off retail sale.
2. **Layer 2 (Cashback Portal / Card Reward):** 2%–5% back via credit card category or shopping portal.
3. **Layer 3 (Discounted Secondary Gift Card):** Paying with a verified digital gift card purchased below face value (e.g. via [CardDeals](https://carddeals.co)).
